## Friday, May 1, 2026: May Day for Legacy SaaS

As we enter May 2026, the transition from "Software as a Service" (SaaS) to **"Agency as a Service" (AaaS)** is reaching an inflection point. The industry is no longer satisfied with tools that help humans work faster; it is demanding agents that do the work autonomously.

### 🌪️ The Headlines

*   **The May Day SaaS Sell-off**: Investment firms are increasingly pricing in the "Agentic Erosion" of traditional SaaS multiples. Platforms that don't have a credible "Agent-First" layer are being treated as legacy debt.
*   **Decentralized Intelligence Swarms**: New reports show a surge in "Swarm Orchestration" tools—small, specialized agents working in concert to replace monolithic department functions. 
*   **AEO Over SEO**: May 1st marks a recorded shift where high-authority domains are seeing up to 40% of their inbound traffic coming via "Agent Referrals" (AI bots fetching data for users) rather than direct human search.

### 🧠 The Agentic Insight
Success in the AaaS era is about **Trust Infrastructure**. When an agent does the work, the human manager needs a way to verify quality without doing the work themselves. This is why we are doubling down on **Reasoning-Ready** content. If an agent can explain *why* it made a decision, the human can scale without losing control.
